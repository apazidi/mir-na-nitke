The separate `idle_game_designer` reviewed runtime, session, UI integration and the agreed pre-code contract. **Conformance PASS**, with 7/7 targeted tests independently run. Full browser playback was not independently repeated by the designer; the separate critic performed that review.

This is conformance to one bounded reinvestment experiment, not proof of enjoyment, long-term return or final artwork approval. The critic's initial art RETURN and subsequent targeted repair are recorded separately; designer agreement does not overrule that gate.
